Terms & disclaimers

Plain-language draft. Have a lawyer review before public launch. This document is part of the open-source project and you should adapt it to your deployment.

1. What JanNidhi Light is

JanNidhi Light is an open-source directory and federation layer, not a data host. It runs a public directory and a one-time human check before a page goes live. The actual page content — bio, payment details, work updates, expenses, donation declarations — lives entirely on the page owner's own backend (their own Supabase project), which they set up and connect themselves. JanNidhi Light never stores, sees, or is able to alter that data.

2. What JanNidhi Light is not

  • Not a payment system. The platform never holds, transmits, routes, collects, escrows or facilitates money. It does not generate payment links or QR codes. A UPI QR image on a page is content uploaded by the page owner, equivalent to a poster on a wall. All payments occur entirely outside the platform, directly between two people.
  • Not a verifier. The one-time human check before a page is listed confirms only that a real person operates it — it is not a legal, professional, or financial verification of anything the page claims.
  • Not a charity, political intermediary, or data processor. Contributions carry no tax benefit of any kind. The platform is not a political party, electoral trust, or fundraising agent, and it does not process or store any page's ledger data.

3. No liability

The platform, its maintainers and hosts accept no liability for: payments sent to anyone listed here; the accuracy of any profile, work update, expense, or donation declaration; impersonation or fraud by users; the security, uptime or configuration of any page owner's own connected backend; tax, FCRA, election-law or other legal consequences for donors or recipients. If you send money to someone on this platform, you are handing money to that person as if in cash. There is no refund, reversal, or recourse through the platform.

4. Obligations of changemakers (page owners)

  • You are solely responsible for the truth of everything on your page, for your own tax treatment, and for the security of your own connected backend and its access credentials. Money received is likely a personal gift; gifts exceeding ₹50,000 per financial year from non-relatives are generally taxable as your income (Section 56(2)(x), Income-tax Act). Keep your own records.
  • Do not accept foreign-sourced funds. Accepting foreign contributions for political activity is prohibited under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010, and the offence is yours, not the platform's.
  • Acknowledge or dispute donor declarations honestly. Uploading fabricated bills or declarations may expose you to legal consequences.
  • Comply with applicable election law, including any restrictions in force during election periods.

5. Obligations of donors

  • Give only your own, Indian-sourced funds, and only if you are an Indian citizen. You confirm this each time you declare a donation.
  • Understand there is no tax benefit and no refund. Verify the payee name in your UPI app matches the name shown on the page before paying.
  • Declaring a donation is voluntary and public (your name can be hidden). Do not declare donations you did not make.

6. Your own connected backend

You choose and operate your own backend (e.g. your own free Supabase project). You are responsible for its configuration, its security, and anyone you give its access key to. JanNidhi Light only stores a pointer to it — your project's URL and public anon key — and connects to it live, in each visitor's own browser, exactly like any other frontend would.

7. Content, takedowns and reports

Maintainers may remove any page from the directory at their discretion — particularly for impersonation, fraud, unlawful content or court/authority direction — and may share data where legally required. Use the report button on any page to flag abuse. Repeated or serious abuse leads to removal.

8. Community volunteers

Every page is reviewed by a volunteer reviewer before appearing in the public Explore directory (a page's own link works immediately regardless — review only gates directory listing). Volunteers are not employees, agents, or representatives of JanNidhi Light. A review decision (approved or flagged) reflects a volunteer's own good-faith judgment, at a point in time, that a real person operates the page — it is not a legal, professional, or platform verification of anything the page's own backend contains.

JanNidhi Light will indemnify and hold harmless any volunteer for a review decision made in good faith and in accordance with its published review guidelines. A volunteer remains personally responsible for any decision made in bad faith, with malicious intent, or clearly outside those guidelines. A reviewer's identity and any private notes are never made public — only the resulting status is.

9. Privacy

Everything you publish on your page is public by design. All of it lives on your own connected backend, not JanNidhi Light's — JanNidhi Light's own database holds only your name, slug, headline, photo, backend pointer, and review status. Anonymous donors' names are never stored anywhere. The project is open source; anyone can run their own instance, and each instance's operator is its own data controller.
