Campaigns

Start a campaign to rally other changemakers around one cause. You become its leader — money for the campaign goes to your own UPI, the same one on your profile. Others request to join; you approve who's actually on your team. Each member keeps their own individual page too — some may still take their own personal support, others might not have a QR of their own and are just part of the team.
