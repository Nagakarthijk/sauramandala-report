About OESN

OESN connects grassroots entrepreneurs with verified service providers — for FSSAI registration, loan facilitation, market linkage, solar installation, and more — through trusted field agents embedded in their communities. Agents earn transparently on verified outcomes, providers get qualified referrals, and entrepreneurs get real results, not just advice.

Currently active in Raichur district, Karnataka, with programmes supported by Anant Foundation and independent agents operating on a pay-per-outcome model.
