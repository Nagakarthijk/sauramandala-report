Why does recording need the screen on?

Browsers (especially iOS Safari) suspend GPS the moment this tab goes to the background, even for an installed app — a platform limit, not a bug here. We request the Screen Wake Lock and show a notification while recording so a dropped signal is visible instead of silent, but there's no way to log GPS with the tab fully backgrounded. If the app closes or crashes mid-walk, reopening it will offer to resume from where it left off.
